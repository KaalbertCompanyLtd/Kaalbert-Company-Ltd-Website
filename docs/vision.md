# Vision — kaalbert.com

## What we are building

kaalbert.com is the qualification-and-authority website for Kaalbert & Company Ltd, a
Ghana-rooted business advisory firm. It is not a brochure site. It is built around two
conversion instruments — a public, scored Business Health Check diagnostic that a founder
can complete in under six minutes and get genuinely useful output from before ever speaking
to a partner, and an Insights publishing engine carrying the firm's own analytical writing —
surrounded by three deep, fee-transparent core offer pages, a capabilities overview, and a
full paid-advertising measurement layer so every enquiry the firm receives can be traced
back to the specific page, article, or advertisement that produced it.

## Why it exists

Most professional-services websites are brochures with a contact form: they describe the
firm, list its services, and wait. They generate a trickle of unqualified enquiries that
cost more partner time to disqualify than they are worth, and their owners conclude that
websites do not work for advisory firms. Kaalbert already owns the two assets that make a
different kind of website work — a named four-stage diagnostic method (Discover, Diagnose,
Design, Deliver) and eight completed Insights articles across two editorial volumes — and
the gap is that neither currently has anywhere to do its work. The site is where those two
assets are put to use: a qualification and authority instrument, not a passive listing.

The opportunity is structural, not cosmetic. The firm's five partners each commit roughly
forty hours a month; a website that pre-qualifies a visitor before any conversation, and
that lets a visitor disqualify themselves if they are not a fit, protects that scarce time
for conversations worth having.

## Who it is for

**Primary — the founder or managing director of a Ghanaian SME**, roughly GHS 500,000 to
GHS 20 million turnover, who needs to recognise their own problem in the site's language,
see evidence the firm has done this before, understand roughly what it costs and how long
it takes, and take a first step that does not feel like a sales call.

**Primary — a finance or operations lead** inside a growing business, checking the firm's
substance (method, deliverables, what it actually produces) before recommending it upward,
not yet the decision-maker but the one who has to be convinced first.

**Secondary — a bank relationship manager, grant body, or development-partner reviewer**,
assessing whether the firm is real, governed, and operating within a defined and stated
professional boundary. This reader disqualifies quickly on vagueness.

**Secondary — a prospective partner, associate, or consultant**, evaluating whether this is
a firm being built deliberately, with a method worth joining.

The site is not written for a general audience. Writing for everyone is the most common way
an advisory website ends up speaking to no one.

## What success looks like at launch

- The eleven-page site, the Business Health Check diagnostic, and three campaign landing
  pages are live at www.kaalbert.com by the end of month five of the firm's year-one plan.
- All eight Section 10 acceptance thresholds pass (performance, page weight, accessibility,
  security, search foundation, content management, analytics, browser/device coverage).
- The Measurement Handover Certificate is signed, confirming all ten Section 11.1
  measurement requirements — tag container, GA4 key events, consent mode, Meta pixel and
  server-side Conversions API, Google Ads import, LinkedIn Insight Tag, domain verification,
  end-to-end attribution, distinct completion states, and tracked WhatsApp — as configured,
  tested, and verified, with the firm holding owner rights to every account.
- A partner with no technical training has successfully published an article, edited page
  copy, updated a published fee range, and created a new landing page from the template,
  unsupervised.

## What success looks like at 6 months

- At least thirty completed diagnostics across the first six months, weighted to the later
  months, with a floor of three in the first full month.
- A completion rate above sixty per cent among visitors who start the diagnostic.
- At least two qualified enquiries reaching a first meeting per month by month six.
- One hundred per cent of enquiries traceable to a source — an untraceable enquiry is
  treated as a build defect, not a marketing gap.
- Steady month-on-month growth in Insights readership and subscription, with at least one
  article ranking on the first page of search results for a specific, problem-shaped query.
- Mobile share of traffic and mobile conversion rate tracked separately from desktop, with
  no material gap between them.

## Where Phase 2 fits

Seven further capabilities — booking, named case studies, a client portal, online payment,
training registration, CRM integration, and the full diagnostic suite as a paid product —
are fully planned in `scope.md`, each against a specific evidence trigger rather than a
date. They are not built at launch, but they are not an afterthought either: Phase 1 is
built with each of their triggers already measurable from day one, and several Phase 1
decisions (the diagnostic scoring engine's generality, the enquiry record's field
structure) are made specifically so meeting a trigger later means building, not
re-planning.
